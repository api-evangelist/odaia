# ODAIA

> ODAIA is the Commercial Intelligence Platform for pharma. It replaces manual analysis and static segmentation with real-time, predictive insights — helping field, marketing, and ops teams identify the right healthcare professionals and act on opportunities faster.

## Platform
- [Platform Overview](https://www.odaia.ai/platform-overview): How ODAIA's commercial intelligence engine unifies sales, marketing, and field data into real-time, actionable insights.
- [Veeva Integration](https://www.odaia.ai/integrations/veeva): How ODAIA connects with Veeva CRM to surface insights directly in existing rep workflows.

## Products
- [ODAIA Field](https://www.odaia.ai/products/field): Real-time HCP prioritization, pre-call insights, and mobile call list access for field reps.
- [Marketing Intelligence](https://www.odaia.ai/products/marketing-intelligence): Campaign performance tracking, HCP engagement insights, and channel optimization for brand teams.
- [Opportunity Analysis](https://www.odaia.ai/opportunity-analysis): Identification, scoring, and prioritization of untapped commercial opportunities.

## Solutions
- [Engagement Intelligence](https://www.odaia.ai/solutions/engagement-intelligence): Solutions for improving HCP engagement quality and coverage.
- [Targeting Intelligence](https://www.odaia.ai/solutions/targeting-intelligence): Dynamic, real-time HCP targeting and segmentation, replacing static annual refreshes.
- [For Brand Marketers](https://www.odaia.ai/solutions/brand-marketers): Campaign intelligence and Rx-focused customer journey optimization for marketing leaders.
